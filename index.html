    How to play

    • On your turn, roll all five dice. Tap any dice you want to hold, then roll the rest. You get three rolls (unless the table says otherwise).
    • After any roll you can stop and score: tap a box on the pad, then tap it again to write the score in. Every turn fills one box, even if it scores nothing.
    • Top section: add up the dice showing that number. Reach 63 across the six boxes for a 35-point bonus (three of each number gets you there).
    • Three or four of a kind: the total of all five dice. Full house, three of one number and two of another: 25. Small straight, four in a row: 30. Large straight, five in a row: 40. Five of a kind: 50. Chance: the total, whatever the dice.
    • Each five of a kind after the first scores a 100-point bonus, as long as the first one scored 50. It still fills a box: its own number’s box if that’s open, otherwise any box in the bottom section, where a full house or straight counts in full.
    • You and your opponent take turns until every box is filled. The higher total wins.
